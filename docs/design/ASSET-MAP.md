# Asset Map

Production imagery is either a supplied restaurant photograph/menu board or an explicitly approved generated brand asset. These two provenance classes are kept distinct in copy and alt text.

- `photos/menu-meals.webp` — current burger/gyro meals board; Gallery and factual menu reference.
- `photos/menu-burgers.webp` — current burgers/chili/melts/dinners board; Story and Gallery.
- `photos/menu-mediterranean.webp` — current wraps/salads/subs/sides board; Story and Gallery.
- `photos/fries.jpg` — customer-contributed fries photo; Home hero and Gallery.
- `photos/fried-mushrooms.jpg` — customer-contributed side photo; Featured Meals and Gallery.
- `photos/storefront.jpg` and `photos/restaurant-front.jpg` — location and Story.

- `brand/food-hero.png` — approved branded marketing composition used in the Home 3D hero; it is not represented as documentary evidence of a specific menu item.
- `brand/food-featured.png` — approved branded marketing composition used for the featured hamburger-meal selection; the semantic price and product name still come from the verified menu transcription.

- `fonts/barlow-condensed-900.woff2`, `fonts/barlow-condensed-700.woff2`, and `fonts/oswald-500.woff2` — local subsets used for stable display and condensed UI typography without remote font requests.

Superseded generated assets (`burger-hero.webp`, `gyro-banner.webp`, `favorites-banner.webp`, and `assets/jumbos-original-*.png`) are intentionally not used.
